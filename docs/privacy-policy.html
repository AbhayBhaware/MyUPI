1. Overview & Core Philosophy

Welcome to MyUPI ("MyUPI: Smart UPI Soundbox", "we", "our", or "us"). MyUPI is designed to transform an Android smartphone into a dedicated payment voice soundbox for Indian shopkeepers, merchants, and small business owners.

Our foundational architectural principle is 100% on-device processing and Zero-PII (Personally Identifiable Information) storage. MyUPI is built to announce incoming payment alerts in real time without sending your personal messages, SMS conversations, or financial transaction logs to external third-party servers.

Zero Financial Data Stored in the Cloud

MyUPI never requests, accesses, or records your bank account numbers, debit/credit card details, UPI PIN, OTPs, or financial credentials. All payment voice alerts are parsed and spoken directly on your device.

2. Sensitive Android Permissions & Justification

In accordance with Google Play's Sensitive Permissions Policy, the following table explicitly explains every permission requested by MyUPI and its strict operational purpose:

Android Permission Classification Exact Purpose & Scope
BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE Special / Sensitive Access Allows MyUPI to detect inbound payment notifications specifically sent by authorized UPI payment apps (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM, Cred, and bank UPI apps). Only the payment amount and sender/app name are extracted to trigger instant audio announcements.
RECEIVE_SMS & READ_SMS High-Risk / Sensitive Permission Serves solely as a backup mechanism to detect official bank credit confirmation SMS messages when push notifications are delayed or unavailable. Personal messages, OTPs, and conversational SMS are strictly ignored and never collected.
REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS System Optimization Ensures the background soundbox listener service remains active and is not prematurely terminated by Android's battery-saving system, guaranteeing that merchants do not miss real-time customer payment alerts.
INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Standard Network Required for user authentication (Firebase Authentication / Google Sign-In) and processing premium subscriptions via the official Google Play Billing system.

3. Information We Collect and Process

A. Information Provided by the User

  • Account Information: When signing in via Google Sign-In or Firebase Authentication, we receive your email address, name, and profile identifier. This is used solely to authenticate your account and manage your active subscription status.
  • App Preferences: Selected announcement language, voice speed, custom business/shop name, and soundbox settings. These preferences are stored locally on your device.

B. Information Processed Locally on Device (Not Uploaded)

  • Payment Notification Content: Text strings received in push notifications from authorized payment apps are parsed on-device to read aloud the received amount.
  • Bank Credit SMS: Inbound SMS messages from authorized banking sender IDs are filtered on-device to detect credit confirmations.
  • Local Transaction History: A rolling local ledger (up to 500 records) is maintained strictly inside the device's local memory (Android SharedPreferences) for the merchant's on-screen review.

C. Automatically Collected Information

  • Device & Diagnostics Information: Anonymous hardware model, operating system version, and general error/crash logs to ensure soundbox stability and troubleshoot device-specific issues.

4. SMS and Notification Processing (Google Play Compliance)

Prominent Disclosure: Notification & SMS Data Handling

MyUPI requires access to Notification Listener Service and SMS (RECEIVE_SMS, READ_SMS) solely to operate as an audio soundbox for business payments.

In adherence to Google Play policies regarding sensitive data:

  1. Strict Allowlist Filtering: The app only listens to notifications coming from a verified allowlist of authorized Indian UPI applications and verified banking sender headers.
  2. Zero Extraction of Private Messages: Messages from messaging apps (e.g., WhatsApp, Telegram, personal SMS), contacts, photos, or personal content are completely ignored and discarded by our system.
  3. No Off-Device Transmission: Extracted transaction amounts and messages are never transmitted to our servers, third-party advertising partners, or any remote database.
  4. No Advertising Use: Your notification and SMS data are never used for targeted advertising, marketing profiles, or data broker sales.

6. Data Security & Storage

We implement commercially acceptable security measures to safeguard your information:

  • Sandboxed Local Storage: All transaction records and user preferences are stored within private, sandboxed Android application storage inaccessible to other apps.
  • Encrypted Connections: Any communication with Firebase or Google Play is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols.
  • Zero Cloud Exposure: Because transaction amounts and customer names are not sent to our cloud servers, they are immune to external server-side data leaks.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

In accordance with Google Play's Account Deletion and Data Deletion Policy, users have full control over the retention and deletion of their data.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your account and associated data at any time through either of the following methods:

  1. Inside the App: Open MyUPI > Go to Settings > Select Account > Tap "Delete My Account". This immediately removes your authentication record and purges all local logs.
  2. By Email Request: Send an email from your registered account to abhaybhaware2001@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Your account and any associated cloud identifiers will be permanently deleted within 7 business days.

When an account deletion is requested:

  • Your Firebase authentication profile (email, name, unique user ID) is permanently deleted.
  • Local transaction records on your phone are cleared whenever you clear app storage or uninstall the application.

8. User Rights and Choices

You have full autonomy over the permissions and data used by MyUPI:

  • Revoking Permissions: You can at any time revoke Notification Access or SMS permissions via Android Settings > Apps > MyUPI > Permissions.
  • Clearing Local Records: You can clear your local transaction history directly from the app's Ledger screen or by clearing the app's cache and data in Android settings.
  • Opting Out: If you do not agree with this policy, you can uninstall the application and discontinue its use.

9. Children's Privacy
